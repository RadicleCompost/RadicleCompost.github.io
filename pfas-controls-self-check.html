PFAS in your organics: a 10-question self-check

Composting doesn't make PFAS. It concentrates what arrives. Councils can't test every pizza box, but they do control the three decisions that matter most: what goes in the bin, what the processing contract requires, and where the product goes. This check shows where your controls are solid and where the gaps are.

This self-check is a general guide, not legal or regulatory advice. PFAS rules differ between states and are changing: Queensland, for example, retired numeric PFAS limits for composting approvals in 2026 and now relies on the general environmental duty. Your obligations depend on your state, your approvals and your contracts.
