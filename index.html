Our Services

Seven distinct service offers, each addressing a specific stage of the composting project lifecycle. Engage us for a single service or combine multiple offers into a tailored package.

SO1

Input Audit & Concept Proposal

Comprehensive situation review — waste stream volumes, site suitability, conceptual layout, cost assessment, and end-market identification. The ideal starting point for any new composting project.

SO2

Construction Planning & Approvals

Coordinating technical experts, navigating government approvals and licensing, developing project budgets, and communicating milestones to your stakeholders and funding bodies.

SO3

Site Construction Project Support

Acting as your expert representative during construction — specifying equipment, negotiating contracts, and overseeing commissioning to ensure the built facility matches the design intent.

SO4

Operations Setup & Training

Standard operating procedures, staff training, quality control systems, and 12 months of troubleshooting and optimisation support as your operation matures.

SO5

Compost Marketing & Sales Strategy

Identifying target markets, developing value propositions, designing trial and demonstration sites, product development advice, and coaching your sales team to build internal capability.

SO6

Quality Testing & Certification

Sampling and testing protocols, interpreting laboratory results, navigating organic certification requirements, and ongoing monitoring to ensure consistent quality and customer confidence.

SO7

3-Year Contracted Expert Support

A long-term advisory partnership with retained access to Radicle Compost expertise across the full project lifecycle. Scheduled site visits, progress reviews, and a dedicated channel for ad-hoc support. Priced by negotiation.

Meet the Team

Martin Tower

Principal Advisor

With over 25 years at the forefront of Australia's recycled organics industry, Martin brings rare depth of practical and strategic expertise to clients navigating the complex world of organic waste processing and resource recovery.

Martin has designed and operated composting facilities across urban and rural settings, working with a diverse range of feedstocks including green waste, FOGO, biosolids, animal manures, cotton trash, and other crop residues. He has managed windrow, aerated floor, and tunnel composting systems, and has a proven track record of lifting operational performance for processing businesses across the sector.

Working with market-leading agronomists, Martin has developed and built markets for compost and compost-derived products across broad-acre agriculture, horticulture, and urban amenity — turning recyclable organics into genuinely profitable revenue streams.

Martin holds an MBA and has completed the Australian Company Directors Course. He has served as a non-executive director on several boards, including the Australian Organics Recycling Association, and is currently Chair of Certified Sustainable Limited.
